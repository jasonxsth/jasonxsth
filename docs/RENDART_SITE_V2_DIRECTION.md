# RENDART site direction

## Position

RENDART translates an idea into a precise, visually convincing system ready for the next stage of implementation. The name is read as research and development through art.

## Audience

- Designers and architects
- Interior brands and manufacturers
- Product, marketing and sales teams

## Information architecture

- `/` editorial overview
- `/portfolio/` real project materials and studies
- `/services/` visualization, design, brand environment and digital processes
- `/about/` method and team model
- `/b2b/` business-facing capabilities
- `/contacts/` first-contact form

## Visual system

- Hero: approved Commissioner and Literata split
- Display type: Geologica Variable, `wght` 350 to 390, `SHRP` 30 to 40
- Body type: Literata Variable, `wght` 360 to 400
- Palette: paper `#F2EADE`, graphite `#403E3E`, teal `#028088`, amber `#F4B41E`, dark `#091416`
- Rhythm: large editorial whitespace, thin rules, asymmetrical media, no generic card grid

## Motion system

- Lenis on desktop fine pointers only, `lerp: 0.1`
- Shared GSAP ticker and ScrollTrigger updates
- One signature aperture section that moves from full-bleed media to a framed rectangle
- Subtle layer movement and media clip reveals
- Native scroll and static media on coarse pointers and reduced-motion devices

## Content constraints

- Do not publish prices until the sales model is confirmed
- Do not publish awards, performance percentages or client outcomes without evidence
- Use real portfolio materials without inventing client names
- Keep the public team description structural until names and roles are confirmed
